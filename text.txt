What was the first step in your E-Commerce Customer Intelligence project?
You should be able to answer something like this:

I started by understanding the data. I used Python and Pandas
 to explore the Olist e-commerce dataset, inspect its structure, identify missing values 
 and duplicate records, and convert timestamps into datetime format.

I also examined the relationships between orders, customers, and purchased items. 
This helped me understand how to connect the datasets and prepare them for customer segmentation 
and business analytics.

That's a meaningful explanation of your work, not just a list of Python functions.
The most important lesson of Day 1: Before building machine learning models or visualizations,
 we must understand what our data represents, assess its quality, and determine how different pieces 
 of information relate to one another.

On Day 2, we'll build directly on this foundation by exploring the remaining datasets
 and preparing them for deeper analysis.
